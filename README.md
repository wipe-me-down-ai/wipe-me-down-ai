1. **Upload two individual photos.** Use a separate, clear, front-facing half-body photo for each person (hair, shoulders and upper-body clothing visible; face unobstructed). Photo 1 is the person on the left at the start; photo 2 is on the right.
2. **Choose the setting.** Pick original kitchen, cozy living room, garage party or city rooftop.
3. **Generate and confirm.** Sign in, confirm the video, and follow progress in [My creations](https://wipemedownai.com/).
4. **Download and share.** Download the finished MP4 within 30 days, then upload it to your preferred social platform.

**Photo requirements:** JPG, PNG or WebP; one person per photo; up to 10 MB each; at least 300 px on each side. Wide or tall photos get margins, never a crop. Avoid distant full-body shots, group photos, sunglasses, heavy filters and tight face-only crops.
