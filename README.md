## How to Make a Wipe Me Down AI Video

1. **Add the two people.** Upload a separate, clear, front-facing half-body photo for each person (hair, shoulders and upper-body clothing visible; face unobstructed). Photo 1 is the person on the left at the start; photo 2 is on the right.
2. **Pick the environment.** Choose original kitchen, cozy living room, garage party or city rooftop.
3. **Sign in and confirm.** Purchase credits if needed, then confirm generation. Photos and the environment can stay as a draft in the current browser across sign-in and payment.
4. **Watch and download.** Follow progress in My creations, then download the finished MP4 within 30 days and upload it to your preferred social platform.

Photos can be **JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB each**. HEIC is not supported. Each side must be at least 300 pixels. Wide or tall photos get margins, never a crop. Prefer evenly lit half-body shots; avoid distant full-body shots, group photos, sunglasses, heavy filters and tight face-only crops. Use only photos you have permission to use.

## Wipe Me Down AI FAQ

### Do I need a CapCut template or an app?

No. Use the [Wipe Me Down AI creator](https://wipemedownai.com/#generator) in a phone or desktop browser. No filming, app install or CapCut template is required.

### Can I use one photo of both of us?

No. The generator needs **two separate individual photos**, one person per image. There is no solo mode.

### Will it use our real voices or my own song?

No. The finished video includes the dance music that comes with the generator. Custom songs, audio uploads and free-form prompts are not supported. Changing the site language or background does not change the music.

### Will the faces and moves match exactly?

The photos guide each person’s appearance and clothing, and the dance follows the Wipe Me Down routine. AI-generated faces, hands, timing and clothing details can vary.

### Can I change the background?

Yes. Choose original kitchen, cozy living room, garage party or city rooftop. The dance and music stay the same.

### Can I close the page while it generates?

Yes, once the video is submitted. Sign back in and check My creations. Processing time varies with the queue.

### Can I download and share the video?

Finished downloads are a full 1080p MP4 without a site watermark. Upload the file yourself to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts or another platform, and follow that platform’s AI-content rules. Downloading does not grant likeness, music or commercial-use rights you do not already have. Completed videos are retained for 30 days after completion.
